Privacy Policy

Effective

In short: we collect what we need to run your account and store the CRM records you enter. Everything is kept in Sydney, Australia, and is never sold. Only you, and your team where your plan shares contacts, can see your contacts. You can export your data or ask us to delete it at any time.

1. About this policy

(ABN ) runs ("the Service"). This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). It covers two kinds of information:

  • Customer information: information about you, the agents and agencies who use the Service.
  • Client information: information about your clients and prospects (vendors, buyers, landlords and so on) that you store in the CRM. We hold this on your behalf. You decide what's collected and how it's used, and you're responsible for your own privacy obligations to those people. We handle it only to provide the Service to you, as this policy describes.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the Service: sign you in, store and sync your CRM, run team features, and show team dashboards.
  • To bill you and manage your subscription.
  • To answer support requests, and to tell you about changes to the Service, your account or these policies.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as keeping tax records.

We don't sell personal information. We don't use client information to contact your clients, for our own marketing, or to train AI models.

4. Who can see it inside the Service

  • Per agent plan: each agent's contacts are private to them. Team owners and admins see only counts (such as number of contacts and calls this week), never client details.
  • Agency plans: the team shares one contact list, so every member of that team can see and change the shared contacts and their activity. Each person's diary, appointments, templates and expenses stay private to them.
  • Our own staff access customer data only when needed to provide support you've asked for, keep the Service secure, or meet a legal requirement.

6. Keeping it safe

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules ensure each account can reach only its own data, or its team's shared list.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • A working copy of your CRM is kept in your browser so it works offline. Sign out on shared computers: signing out removes that copy from the device.
  • If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll notify affected customers and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires. We'll help agencies meet their own notification duties to their clients.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. After an account or subscription ends, we keep it for 90 days so you can return or export it, and then delete it. Invoices and payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires (usually five years). Deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for a short time until they're replaced.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: you can see and change your account details and CRM records in the app at any time.
  • Export: download everything from the account menu (Download my data).
  • Delete: use Delete my account in the account menu to delete your account and your data straight away, or email and we'll do it for you and confirm when it's done.
  • You can also ask us what personal information we hold about you. We'll respond within 30 days.

9. If you're a client of an agent who uses this Service

The agent or agency you dealt with decides what information about you is stored and how it's used. Please contact them first to access, correct or delete it. If you contact us, we'll pass your request to them and help them respond.

If you have a privacy concern, email . We'll acknowledge it within 5 business days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

We may update this policy. For significant changes we'll tell you by email or in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
