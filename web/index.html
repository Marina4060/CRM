What it does

Built around how agents actually prospect

Not a general sales CRM with property fields added. MICRM starts from the street you are working today.

Your patch, street by street

Bring in owners from your RP Data lists. Filter by suburb and street, and drag each owner along as the conversation moves.

  • Street and house-number order, so you can call or doorknock down a street
  • A map of every owner, coloured by stage, with "Who's within 300 m?" around a new listing
  • The Call Runner takes you through your list one owner at a time, with their history beside you
  • Do not call flags, and a record of every call, text and email

Buyers who qualify themselves

Type what a buyer tells you, the way you would write it in your notebook. MICRM reads each note and checks them again.

  • "Pre-approved to $1.1m, first home buyer, ready now" moves them to Qualified
  • "Needs to sell first" moves them back, and the card says why
  • Tag buyers to your listings, and add open home visitors in one tap

A vendor report every week, in minutes

Open home numbers and buyer feedback come straight from your open home register. Add the week's online views and enquiries, then send it.

  • Days on market, groups through, views, enquiries and the buyer price range
  • Branded with your name, agency, colour and logo
  • Email it, text a summary, or save it as a PDF. Buyer names never appear in it.

Map view

Every owner on a street map, coloured by stage. Find who lives within 300 m of your new listing.

SMS and email templates

Just listed, just sold and appraisal follow-ups that fill in the owner's name and address, signed with your details.

Diary, calendar and Today

Callbacks, appraisals and open homes, and a list each morning of who to contact today.

Expenses, receipts and BAS

Photograph a receipt as you pay. GST is worked out and your quarterly BAS figures are ready for your accountant.

Logbook

Record business trips for your car claim without a separate app.

Teams and agencies

Owner, admin and agent roles. On an agency plan the whole office shares one contact list, and the dashboard shows each agent's activity.

The rules you prospect under

Built for Australian agents, with Western Australia in mind

MICRM helps you keep good records, but you stay responsible for following the law when you call, text or email. These are the main rules to know.

Do Not Call Register

Before cold calling, check numbers against the Do Not Call Register unless you have the person's consent. MICRM lets you mark people as Do not call and shows it on their card and in your call list, but it does not check numbers against the Register for you.

Calling hours

Telemarketing calls can be made on weekdays from 9am to 8pm and on Saturdays from 9am to 5pm, and not on Sundays, in the time where the person lives. For owners in Western Australia that is Perth time.

SMS and email

Under the Spam Act 2003, marketing messages need the person's consent, must say who you are and must offer a working way to unsubscribe. MICRM signs your templates with your details; add your unsubscribe wording to each template.

Privacy

Handle owners' and buyers' details under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply to you. Buyer scores in MICRM are a guide for you only; they make no decisions on their own.

Western Australian agents

Licensed agents and sales representatives in WA work under the Real Estate and Business Agents Act 1978 and the Real Estate and Business Agents and Sales Representatives Code of Conduct 2016, which among other things requires passing every offer, including verbal offers, to the seller.

Property data

Only import owner lists you are licensed to use, such as under your CoreLogic RP Data subscription, and remove people who ask you to.

This is general information, not legal advice. Check the Australian Communications and Media Authority (acma.gov.au), the OAIC (oaic.gov.au) and Consumer Protection WA (consumerprotection.wa.gov.au) for the current rules.

Questions

Before you start

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. Sign up with your email and use everything for 14 days. You only add a card if you decide to subscribe.

Can I bring in my RP Data lists?

Yes. Paste or import your RP Data owner lists and MICRM sorts them into suburbs and streets for you.

How does the desktop download work?

While you are on the trial or subscribed, open Help in MICRM and choose Download MICRM. It saves one file to your computer that opens in your browser, even without internet. The desktop copy keeps its data on that computer, so use Backup to move data between it and your online account.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Open MICRM in Safari or Chrome and add it to your home screen. It fits phone screens and syncs with your computer.

Can my whole office use it?

Yes. Choose Agency 10 or Agency 20 and everyone works from one shared contact list. On the Per agent plan each agent's contacts stay private.

Does MICRM check the Do Not Call Register?

No. MICRM stores the Do not call status you give each person and shows it wherever you see them, but it does not check numbers against the Register. Wash your lists through the Register (see donotcall.gov.au) before calling people you don't have consent from.

What happens to my data if I stop?

You can still sign in and download everything. After 90 days your data is deleted, as our privacy policy explains.
