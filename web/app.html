MICRM on your desktop

Download MICRM as a single file and open it in your browser, even without internet. It comes with your trial or subscription.

The desktop copy keeps its contacts in the browser you open it in, separate from your online account. Use its Backup button, and Import file to move data between them.

Common questions

Is my data saved if I lose my phone?

Yes. Everything you do in the CRM is saved to your account in the cloud as you work. Sign in on any device to carry on.

Can I use it on my phone like an app?

What if I have no signal?

Keep working. Changes are kept on the device and sent to the cloud as soon as you're back online. The sync dot at the top shows the status.

Can my principal see my clients?

On the Per agent plan, no: owners and admins see pipeline numbers and activity counts, never client names or details. On the Agency plans the whole team works one shared contact list, so everyone in the team sees those contacts. Your diary, appointments, templates and expenses always stay personal.

What's the difference between the plans?

Per agent ($100 AUD per agent per month): each agent has their own private contacts. Agency 10 ($500 AUD per month, up to 10 people) and Agency 20 ($1000 AUD per month, up to 20 people): one shared contact list for the whole agency, so anyone can pick up a lead and see every call, text and note on it. The team owner can switch plans under Billing.

How does billing work?

How do I cancel?

The team owner opens Billing → Manage billing and cancels. The team keeps access until the end of the paid month. You can download your data at any time from the account menu.

The CRM's own Backup button

Still works, and is a good habit before big imports. It saves a file to your device; your cloud copy is separate.

Delete my account

This permanently deletes your login, your contacts and notes, your diary and other CRM data, your dashboard figures and your support messages. It can't be undone.

Your subscription is cancelled straight away. The rest of this month isn't refunded, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires it.

Contacts you added to an agency's shared list stay with the agency. Invoices stay with Stripe, as tax law requires.
